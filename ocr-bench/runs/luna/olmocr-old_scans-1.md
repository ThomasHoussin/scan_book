Bangor. Pa. May 22nd 1914.

Col. Roosevelt Comrade & Friend

Dear Sir

I am one of Gen D.E. Sickles old Regts Brigade I Served through all of the Civil War. During the 5. years was with Gen Sickles when He lost His leg, then He left us. I Regret your Absence from this country. During your trip to S.A. I saw Mr Pinchott Here & Had a little talk with him about you & the Political Situation of the country I am with you for the Election of Gifford Pinchott as U.S. Senator. One Law Here must Be Repealed. as we are Deprived of Voting for whoever we Desire at the primaries. if you come to Easton or Bangor Pa I would like to See you. Address 25 Market St. Bangor Pa

Yours Very Resp..  
Caleb Aven
