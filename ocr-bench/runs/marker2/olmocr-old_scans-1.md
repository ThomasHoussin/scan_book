MAY 28 ARST

31.

Bangor Pa. May 22<sup>nd</sup> 1914.  
Col. Rosevelt & Comrade & Friend

I am one of D. E. Sickles old  
Regt & Brigade I served through  
all of the civil War. During the  
5. years was with Gen Sickles  
when He lost His log. then He  
left us. I regret you & assure from  
this country During your trip to  
S. A. I saw me Pfnchott Here &  
Had a solite talk with Him about  
you & the Political Situation of the  
country I am with you for the  
Election of Eifford Pfnchott as U.S.  
Senitor. one loaw Here must Be  
Repealed as we are Defined of  
Voting for whoever we desire at  
the Primaries. if you come to Easton  
or Bangor Pa I would site to  
See you Address 25 market St Bangor  
Yours very Resp. Caleb Ahen
