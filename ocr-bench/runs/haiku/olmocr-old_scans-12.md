Hoboken 5-22-14

Mrs Theodore Roosevelt
Dear Madam.

I know you will forgive the liberty I am taking when I explain my motive. I want Mr Roosevelt to take Warburg's Tincture for his jungle fever, and as I am a married man, take this means of bringing it to his notice. It can be procured of any first class drug-store.
